A Google Ads promo code gives new advertisers ad credit after they spend a set amount within a time limit, usually 60 days. The offer amount varies by country. The credit is not free money up front: you spend first, Google checks you are genuinely new, then adds the credit, which you must use before it expires.

This guide explains how a Google Ads promo code works, the rules in the terms, and how to avoid losing the credit.

## How the new advertiser offer works

1. **Redeem the offer.** Many offers are applied automatically when you sign up; some need a promotional code
2. **Spend the required amount** on ads within the time limit, typically 60 days
3. **Verification.** After you reach the spend, Google can take up to 35 days to confirm you are new to Google Ads
4. **Credit applied.** The credit appears in your billing and is used against future ad costs
5. **Use it before it expires,** usually 60 days after it is applied

You can check the status on the Promotions page under Billing.

## Rules that catch people out

- **You must be new.** Offers are for first-time advertisers. Opening another account does not make you new again
- **Timing matters.** The terms require the code to be entered within a short window, often within 14 days of your first ad impression, so apply it early
- **Spending is what counts.** Paying money into the account is not the same as spending it on ads
- **Taxes and fees do not count** towards the spend requirement, and credit cannot pay them
- **One offer per customer**
- **Past costs are not covered.** Credit only applies to advertising after the code is applied
- **No refunds.** Unused credit cannot be refunded if it expires or you close the account
- **Ads keep running after the credit is used up,** and you are charged normally. Google does not always notify you when it runs out

Google's support team cannot give promo codes on request. Offers come through Google's own marketing, partners and services that include Google Ads credit.

## Google Partner offers for new clients

Google Partners and Premier Partners can access promotional offers for eligible new client accounts, often within a short window after the account is created. If you are about to open your first Google Ads account with a partner's help, ask whether a partner offer is available before you start spending.

## Do not let the credit drive bad decisions

A Google Ads promo code is a bonus, not a strategy. Spending fast just to reach the threshold, on broad keywords with no tracking, can cost more than the credit is worth. Set up conversion tracking first, launch a focused campaign, and let the credit reduce the cost of learning. ## Other kinds of Google Ads credit

Not every credit is a promotion. Google also issues credits for invalid traffic found after billing, shown as "Invalid activity" in your billing adjustments. [Click fraud in Google Ads](/learn/google-ads-invalid-traffic-click-fraud/) explains those.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get a Google Ads promo code?

New advertiser offers are often applied automatically when you create your first account, or come through Google's marketing, Google Partners and services that include Google Ads credit. Google support cannot provide codes on request.

### How long does it take to get Google Ads promotional credit?

After you meet the spend requirement, Google can take up to 35 days to verify you are a new advertiser before the credit is added.

### Can existing advertisers use a Google Ads promo code?

New advertiser offers are only for first-time advertisers. Google occasionally offers credits to existing advertisers for trying new features or reactivating accounts, under separate terms.

### Does Google Ads promotional credit expire?

Yes. Credit typically expires 60 days after it is applied, and unused credit is not refunded.

### Why didn't I receive my Google Ads credit?

Common reasons are not being a new advertiser, entering the code too late, not reaching the spend within the time limit, or counting payments and taxes as spend. Check the Promotions page under Billing.
