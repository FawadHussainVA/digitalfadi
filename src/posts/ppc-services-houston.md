## What makes Houston different

- **A very large metro.** Houston and its suburbs, from Katy and Sugar Land to The Woodlands and Pearland, cover a huge area. Customers rarely want a business an hour away in traffic, so tight location targeting matters
- **Weather-driven demand.** Heavy storms, flooding and hurricane season create sudden spikes for restoration, roofing, plumbing, tree services and generators. Campaigns should be ready to scale when weather hits, and pause when crews are fully booked
- **Heat and humidity.** HVAC, pest control and mould remediation demand stays high for much of the year
- **Energy, medical and industrial sectors.** The city's energy industry and large medical centre bring business-to-business and healthcare searches with long decision cycles
- **Bilingual searches.** Many Houston customers search in Spanish, and Vietnamese and other languages are common in some areas. [The Google Ads language setting update](/learn/google-ads-language-setting-update/) explains how Google handles language now

## What good PPC services in Houston include

1. **Accurate conversion tracking,** with calls, forms and bookings matched to what your team received
2. **Location targeting** by suburb or radius around where you work, set to people in your area. [The location setting that wastes budget](/learn/google-ads-location-targeting-settings/) explains the common mistake
3. **Separate campaigns** for emergency and planned work, so storm demand does not drain the budget for routine jobs
4. **Weekly search term reviews** and negative keywords
5. **Clear reporting** on leads or sales and their cost, not just clicks

## Storm season: be ready before it starts

For restoration, roofing and plumbing businesses, the weeks after a major storm can decide the year. PPC services in Houston should plan for it:

- Emergency campaigns built and paused in advance, ready to switch on
- Budgets that can be raised quickly when demand spikes
- Ads that state availability honestly, so you do not pay for calls you cannot take
- Call tracking that shows which calls became jobs

[Google Ads for water damage restoration](/google-ads-for-water-damage-restoration/) and [Google Ads for roofing contractors](/google-ads-for-roofing-contractors/) cover these trades in detail.

## What PPC services cost in Houston

You pay Google directly for clicks, plus a management fee if someone runs the account. Fees are usually a flat monthly amount, a percentage of ad spend, or a project fee. Competitive Houston searches, such as personal injury law and emergency services, can cost a lot per click, which is why tracking cost per customer matters more than the click price. [Google Ads management cost](/learn/google-ads-management-cost/) explains the pricing models.

## Choosing who provides PPC services in Houston

Ask any Houston provider, local or remote:

- Will the Google Ads account be in my business's name?
- How will you track calls and bookings, not just clicks?
- How will you handle storm season and seasonal demand?
- What will I see each month, and who makes the changes?
