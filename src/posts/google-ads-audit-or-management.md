An audit tells you what is wrong with your Google Ads account. Ongoing management fixes it and keeps improving the account every week. Choose an audit if you want to know where you stand, or plan to run the account yourself or keep your current provider. Choose management if you want someone to take responsibility for results.

Many businesses start with an audit, because it shows how much work the account really needs before committing to a monthly fee.

## What an audit gives you

An audit is a one-off review of the account. A good one checks, in this order:

1. **Conversion tracking:** whether the account measures real leads and sales
2. **Search terms:** where the money actually goes
3. **Structure and settings:** campaigns, locations, devices, schedules and bidding
4. **Ads and landing pages:** whether they match what people search for

You end up with a list of what is wrong and what matters most. You can fix it yourself, give it to your current manager, or use it to compare providers. [Google Ads audit checklist](/learn/google-ads-audit-checklist/) shows the checks in detail.

## What management gives you

Management is ongoing work on the account:

- Fixing the problems an audit would find
- Reviewing search terms and adding negatives every week or two
- Adjusting bids, budgets and structure as results come in
- Testing ads and landing pages
- Keeping tracking working after website changes
- Reporting what changed, why, and what happens next

The value is in the weeks and months after the first fixes, as the account keeps improving.

## Which one fits your situation

| Your situation | Start with |
|---|---|
| You do not know whether the account is set up properly | An audit |
| You plan to run the account yourself | An audit with solutions |
| You have a manager or agency and want a second opinion | An audit |
| Results dropped and you need to know why before deciding | An audit |
| You want someone to take responsibility for results | Management |
| You are launching a new account and have no time to run it | Management |
| Your spend is growing and needs active control | Management |

## Free audit, paid analysis or management

My own options work as steps, so you only pay for what you need:

- **Free Google Ads audit:** a list of the issues in your account, at no cost and with no obligation
- **Account analysis, $100 one-time:** every issue found with the solution for each one, in priority order, in writing, with a walkthrough call, delivered within 3 days
- **Management, $499 a month,** or **10% of managed ad spend** for larger accounts: full management, including tracking, weekly optimisation, testing and a monthly review call

If the analysis shows a handful of fixes, you may not need management at all. If it shows deeper problems, you know exactly what management would involve. [Google Ads management cost](/learn/google-ads-management-cost/) explains how management is usually priced.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between a Google Ads audit and management?

An audit is a one-off review that tells you what is wrong. Management is ongoing work that fixes the problems and keeps improving the account every week.

### Do I need an audit before hiring a Google Ads manager?

It is not required, but it helps. An audit shows how much work the account needs, so you can compare proposals properly and check that a manager is fixing the right things.

### Can I use an audit if I already have an agency?

Yes. An independent audit is a good way to check whether your agency is working on the right things. Share the findings with them and see how they respond.

### What does the free audit include compared with the paid analysis?

The free audit lists the issues in your account. The $100 analysis adds the solution for each issue, in priority order and in writing, with a walkthrough call, delivered within 3 days.

### How long does a Google Ads audit take?

It depends on the size of the account. My account analysis is delivered within 3 days of receiving access.
