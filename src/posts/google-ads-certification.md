Google Ads certification is a free qualification from Google that shows someone has passed an online exam on a part of Google Ads, such as Search, Display, Video or Shopping. You earn it through Google's Skillshop platform, and it lasts about a year. It proves knowledge of how the platform works, but not that someone can run a profitable account.

This guide explains how to get Google Ads certification and what it means when you are choosing who manages your ads.

## The Google Ads certifications

Google offers certifications for the main parts of Google Ads, including:

- **Search:** keywords, ads, bidding and Search campaigns
- **Display:** image ads and audiences across websites and apps
- **Video:** YouTube advertising
- **Shopping:** Merchant Center and Shopping campaigns
- **Apps:** app install and engagement campaigns
- **Measurement:** conversion tracking and attribution

Google also adds newer certifications as products change, such as courses on its AI-powered campaign types.

## How to get Google Ads certification

1. Create a free Skillshop account with your Google account
2. Choose a certification and work through the learning path
3. Take the assessment, which is a timed multiple-choice exam
4. Pass to earn the certification and a shareable certificate
5. Retake the assessment when it expires, usually after a year

There is no fee, and you can retake an exam if you do not pass.

## What Google Ads certification proves

- The person knows the platform's features and terms
- They studied recent versions of Google's products
- They passed an exam within the last year

## What it does not prove

- That they can build an account structure that makes money
- That they understand your industry, customers or margins
- That they set up conversion tracking correctly
- That they will review your account regularly

Thousands of people hold Google Ads certification. It is a sensible minimum, not a sign of results.

## Certification vs Google Partner status

These are different things:

- **Google Ads certification** belongs to a person and comes from passing exams
- **Google Partner and Premier Partner** status belongs to a company or freelancer, and Google awards it based on certification, client spend, performance and growth across managed accounts. Premier Partner is the higher level, given to a small share of partners each year

When hiring, partner status tells you more about real account management than a single certificate does. You can check it in Google's Partners directory.

## What to ask when hiring

Instead of relying on Google Ads certification alone, ask:

- What would you check first in my account?
- How will you measure success: leads, bookings or sales?
- Can I see examples of results in similar businesses?
- Will the account stay in my name?

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Google Ads certification free?

Yes. Google Ads certification is free through Google's Skillshop platform, including the learning materials and the exam.

### How long does Google Ads certification last?

Most Google Ads certifications are valid for about a year, after which you retake the assessment to keep them current.

### Is Google Ads certification worth it?

For people learning Google Ads, yes, because it gives a structured introduction. For businesses hiring, it is a useful minimum but not proof that someone can run a profitable account.

### What is the difference between Google Ads certification and Google Partner?

Certification is an individual qualification from passing exams. Partner and Premier Partner status are awarded to companies or freelancers based on certification, client spend, performance and growth.

### Should I hire someone just because they are Google Ads certified?

No. Use certification as a minimum, then ask how they would approach your account, how they measure results, and whether you keep ownership of the account.
