Performance Max asset A/B testing lets you test a change to an asset group before it replaces your current creative. The newest version sits on the **Edit assets** page: a panel called **Save and test asset group changes** turns your edits into an A/B test against the original assets, instead of applying them straight away.

By default, if the new version wins, its changes are applied to the original asset group. While the test runs, the asset group is locked and cannot be edited.

## Why Performance Max asset A/B testing matters

Before this, changing assets in Performance Max meant replacing what was there and hoping results improved. Because Performance Max mixes assets automatically and many other things change at the same time, it was hard to know whether a new headline or image actually helped.

Google rolled out A/B testing for Performance Max asset groups in October 2026, letting advertisers test changes to headlines, descriptions, images and videos. In November 2026, it added the option to start that test directly from the asset group editor.

## How it works

1. Open your Performance Max campaign and go to **Asset groups**
2. Choose an asset group and select **Edit assets**
3. Make your changes: new headlines, descriptions, images or videos
4. At the bottom of the page, in **Save and test asset group changes**, switch on **Save asset group changes as an A/B test**
5. Review the test settings and save

Your original assets become the **control**, and your edited version becomes the **treatment**. Traffic is split between them so the results can be compared.

## The rule that catches people out: the asset lock

Once the experiment starts, you **cannot edit, add or remove any assets** in the asset group being tested until the experiment ends. The editing pages for that group become view-only.

Plan around this:

- Finish all creative work before starting the test
- Do not start a test just before a sale or launch that needs new assets
- If an urgent change is needed, you will have to end the experiment first

## What happens when the test ends

By default, Google applies the treatment's changes to the original asset group if the treatment performs better. You can change this setting if you prefer to review the results and apply changes yourself, which is often the safer choice for brand-sensitive creative.

## What to test with Performance Max asset A/B testing

Test one idea at a time, so the result is clear:

- **A new message:** price-led headlines against benefit-led headlines
- **Image style:** product images against lifestyle images, or polished brand creative against customer-style content
- **Adding video** where the asset group has none
- **Offers:** with and without a promotion in the headline

Avoid changing everything at once. If the treatment wins, you will not know which change made the difference. [How to write Google Ads headlines](/learn/how-to-write-google-ads-headlines/) covers writing distinct headlines worth testing.

## How long to run it

Give the test enough time and conversions to produce a reliable result. Several weeks is common, and longer for low-volume accounts or long sales cycles. Do not judge it in the first few days, when results are noisy. [Google Ads experiments](/learn/google-ads-experiments-ab-testing-guide/) explains how to read test results fairly.

## Other recent asset editing features

Google has added several related tools to Performance Max asset groups:

- **Copy and edit with AI** (beta), which duplicates an asset group and helps you revise its creative
- **Apply theme to asset group,** which creates a seasonal version of a high-performing asset group and leaves the original untouched

These help you create variations; Performance Max asset A/B testing tells you whether those variations actually perform better.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Performance Max asset A/B testing?

It is a Google Ads feature that tests changes to a Performance Max asset group, such as new headlines, images or videos, against the original assets, so you can see whether the changes improve results before applying them.

### How do I start a Performance Max asset A/B test from the Edit assets page?

Edit the asset group, then switch on "Save asset group changes as an A/B test" in the Save and test asset group changes panel at the bottom of the Edit assets page, and save.

### Can I edit assets during a Performance Max A/B test?

No. Once the experiment starts, the tested asset group is locked. You cannot edit, add or remove assets until the experiment ends.

### What happens when a Performance Max asset test ends?

By default, the treatment's changes are applied to the original asset group if they perform better. You can change this to review and apply changes yourself.

### How long should a Performance Max asset A/B test run?

Long enough to gather a reliable number of conversions, usually several weeks. Low-volume accounts and long sales cycles need longer.
