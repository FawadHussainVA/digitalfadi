## How to fill it in

### Step 1: set the context

Fill in the account details first. The target is the most important field: a cost per lead of 80 is excellent for one business and a disaster for another. If you do not know what you can afford, the [metrics calculator](/tools/metrics-calculator/) and [break-even ROAS calculator](/tools/break-even-roas-calculator/) work it out.

### Step 2: work through the sections in order

Start with conversion tracking and do not skip ahead. If tracking is wrong, the numbers you use to judge everything else are wrong too. Mark each check:

- **OK** when it is set up correctly
- **Fix** when it is worth improving but not costing money today
- **Critical** when it is costing money right now

Write what you actually found in the notes column, not just a tick. "47 search terms containing 'jobs' spent 310 last quarter" is far more useful later than "negatives missing".

### Step 3: score each section

Count the marks on the score summary page. A section full of Critical marks is where to start, whatever else the account does well.

### Step 4: cost the biggest issues

Move every Critical item, and the most important Fix items, to the Top issues table, and estimate a monthly cost for each:

- **Wasted spend:** add up the cost of search terms, placements or locations that never convert
- **Missed results:** if a profitable campaign loses impression share to budget, roughly estimate the extra conversions a bigger budget would bring at the current conversion rate
- **Measurement errors:** when tracking is wrong, note that the true cost is unknown, which is itself the reason to fix it first

The figures do not need to be exact. They exist so the fixes can be ranked by money rather than by opinion.

### Step 5: build the action plan

Sort the fixes into three stages:

1. **This week:** tracking errors, obvious wasted spend, broken pages and forms
2. **This month:** structure, negative keyword lists, bidding and budget changes
3. **Next quarter:** testing ads, landing pages and new campaigns

Then make one change at a time where you can, so you can see what each one did. [How long does it take for Google Ads to work?](/learn/how-long-does-google-ads-take-to-work/) explains how long to wait before judging.
