## What makes Dallas different for Google Ads

- **A two-city metro.** Dallas, Fort Worth and suburbs such as Plano, Frisco, McKinney and Arlington form one of the largest metros in the country. Customers in Frisco rarely want a provider in south Dallas, so location targeting needs to follow real service areas
- **Fast-growing suburbs.** New housing in the northern suburbs brings steady demand for home services, moving, property management and new-resident searches
- **Corporate headquarters and business services.** Many companies are based in the area, which brings business-to-business searches with longer sales cycles and higher values
- **Competitive local services.** Home services, legal and healthcare searches attract many advertisers, so structure and tracking decide who wins profitably
- **Weather swings.** Hail storms and summer heat create spikes for roofing, auto glass, restoration and HVAC

## What a good PPC company in Dallas, or freelancer, should do

1. **Check tracking first,** and confirm calls, forms and sales match your own records
2. **Target real service areas,** by city or radius, with people in the area rather than people interested in it. [The location setting that wastes budget](/learn/google-ads-location-targeting-settings/) explains why
3. **Separate campaigns** by service and value, so cheap searches do not take the budget for profitable work
4. **Review search terms** and add negative keywords every week or two
5. **Report what changed and why,** with leads or sales and their cost

## Questions to ask a PPC company in Dallas

- Will the Google Ads account be registered to my business, with my payment method?
- What will you check in my account first?
- How do you measure success: leads, booked jobs, sales or revenue?
- How will you handle hail and heat season if demand spikes?
- Who will work on my account, and how often?
- What are the contract terms and notice period?

## Red flags

- You cannot see the account, or the company owns it
- Reports show clicks and impressions but not leads or sales
- Guaranteed results before anyone has looked at your account
- Ad spend invoiced through the company with an unclear markup
- Long contracts with no way out

## What a PPC company in Dallas or a freelancer costs

Ad spend should be paid directly to Google from your own account. Management is usually a flat monthly fee, a percentage of ad spend or a project fee. [Google Ads management cost](/learn/google-ads-management-cost/) compares the models and lists what should be included.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I choose a PPC company in Dallas?

Check that the account stays in your name, that tracking measures leads or sales, that the company understands DFW service areas, and that you will see what changes each month. Ask what they would check first in your account.

### How much does a PPC company in Dallas charge?

Most charge a flat monthly fee, a percentage of ad spend, or a project fee. Your ad spend should go directly to Google. Compare what each company does each month, not just the price.

### What are the red flags when hiring a PPC company?

Not owning your account, reports without leads or sales, guaranteed results, ad spend markups and long contracts with no exit.

### Does a PPC company need to be in Dallas?

No. Google Ads is managed online, so experience, tracking and communication matter more than location.

### What should a PPC company do in the first month?

Check tracking, review search terms and structure, fix location and schedule settings, and agree how success will be measured. [What to expect in the first 90 days](/learn/first-90-days-of-google-ads-management/) covers the full timeline.
