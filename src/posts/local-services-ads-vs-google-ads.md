Local service businesses are regularly told to choose between Local Services Ads and ordinary Google Ads, as though they compete. They do not. They occupy different parts of the results page, charge on different models, and suit different parts of the same business.

The question worth asking is not which one. It is what each is for and how to tell them apart in reporting.

## What actually differs

**Position.** Local Services Ads sit at the very top, above the normal text adverts and above the map pack.

**Charging.** Local Services Ads charge per lead. Google Ads charges per click. You pay for a call or message rather than for somebody arriving on your site.

**Verification.** Local Services Ads require licence and insurance checks and a background check in many categories. It takes time and it is also a barrier to your competitors.

**Control.** Google Ads gives you keywords, adverts, landing pages and schedules. Local Services Ads gives you services, areas, hours and budget. Much less to tune.

**Destination.** A Local Services Ad produces a call or message. A Google Ad sends somebody to your website, where you control what they see.

## Where Local Services Ads win

- You are charged for a lead rather than a click, so unqualified clicks cost nothing
- The position is above everything else
- The Google Guaranteed badge carries weight with people choosing a stranger to enter their home
- Disputes let you reclaim money for calls that were not genuine enquiries
- Less to manage day to day

## Where ordinary Google Ads win

- Services and search terms outside the Local Services categories
- Anything needing explanation before a call, which means a landing page
- Full control of keywords, negatives, schedules and geography
- Remarketing to people who visited and did not call
- Shopping, if the business also sells products

## Why most businesses should run both

They cover different searches and different stages.

Local Services Ads capture people ready to call somebody now. Google Ads captures people comparing, researching, or searching something outside the Local Services categories.

Running both also protects more of the page. If you hold the Local Services slot and a text advert, a competitor has less room above your organic listing.

The budget split depends on category and competition. A sensible starting point is to fund Local Services Ads to the level where the leads are good and the disputes are manageable, then put the rest into Search where you can control the message.

## The part people skip: disputing leads

Local Services Ads bills per lead, and not every lead is genuine. Wrong numbers, suppliers, people outside your area, somebody asking for a service you do not offer.

Google allows disputes. Businesses that do not dispute pay for all of it and let their own cost per job drift upwards unchallenged.

Make it a weekly habit. It is the single highest return admin task in a Local Services account.

## Ranking in Local Services Ads

It is not an auction in the Google Ads sense. Position is influenced by review score and volume, responsiveness to leads, proximity, hours, and your budget.

Two of those are operational rather than advertising. Answering quickly and collecting reviews moves position more reliably than raising budget.

## Measuring them apart

The common mistake is blending the two into one cost per lead.

They produce different things. A Local Services lead is a call. A Google Ads conversion might be a form fill, a call or a page visit. Averaging them produces a number that describes neither.

Report separately, and judge both on cost per booked job rather than cost per lead. That requires feeding job outcomes back into the account, which is covered in [why your Google Ads calls are not becoming jobs](/learn/why-google-ads-calls-are-not-becoming-jobs/).

## If you can only start with one

Start with Local Services Ads if your category is covered, you can pass verification, and you have reviews. The barrier to entry works in your favour and the lead-based charging is forgiving while you learn.

Start with Google Ads if your category is not covered, your service needs explaining before somebody calls, or you need control over exactly which searches you appear for.
