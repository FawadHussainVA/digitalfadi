Local Services Ads are a separate format from standard Search campaigns. They appear at the top of Google results for local service categories, show the business name, rating and availability, and charge per lead rather than per click. For HVAC companies, they can be a strong source of phone calls, but they work differently enough that they should be evaluated on their own terms.

## How they differ from Search

In a standard Search campaign, you choose keywords, write ads, and pay each time someone clicks. In Local Services Ads, Google manages the placement and the ad format. You set a budget and a service area, choose which services you offer, and pay for leads that Google judges to be genuine enquiries.

Because the format is different, your keyword lists, negative keywords and landing pages do not carry over. Local Services Ads have their own settings, and a Search account that performs well is not automatically a guide to how Local Services Ads will perform.

## Verification comes first

Businesses that want to appear in Local Services Ads go through a verification process. This typically involves checking the company's licences and insurance, along with background checks on the business. The exact requirements depend on the service and the location, and they can change, so check the current requirements in your Google Local Services account before you commit time to the application.

Keep your licence details, insurance certificates and business registration documents ready. A delayed verification means lost visibility while competitors are running.

## Pricing and lead quality

Local Services Ads charge for leads, and the definition of a chargeable lead matters. Some enquiries that look like leads may not be charged, and some that are charged may not become jobs. Track the leads you receive, mark which ones booked, and check whether the cost per booked job is acceptable compared with your other channels.

Respond quickly. Lead responsiveness can affect how Google ranks and presents your business, and a missed call is money already spent. Set up a process so every lead is answered during working hours, and decide in advance who handles out-of-hours enquiries.

## Reviews and profile

Reviews are a major part of how Local Services Ads present a business. Ask recent customers for reviews through a simple, consistent process, and reply to every review, positive or negative. Keep your service area accurate, because leads from outside your coverage waste budget and create frustration on both sides.

## Should you use them?

Local Services Ads suit many HVAC companies, especially those with a clear local service area and the ability to answer calls quickly. They are worth testing alongside Search campaigns, with a clear way to compare cost per booked job. If your team cannot respond to leads promptly, or your service area is very wide, Search campaigns may give you more control.
