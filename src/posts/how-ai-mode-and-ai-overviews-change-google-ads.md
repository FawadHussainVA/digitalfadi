Google's shift to AI Overviews and AI Mode changes where ads appear, not how you buy them. Your existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns are already eligible to show inside and around these AI answers, through the same auction and bidding, at no extra cost. What decides whether your ads appear inside an AI answer is mainly your match types: campaigns using only exact and phrase match can show above and below an AI Overview, but not inside it. You cannot target or opt out of these placements separately, and their results are mixed into your normal reports.

## What AI Overviews and AI Mode are

- **AI Overviews** are the AI-written summaries that appear at the top of many Google search results, answering the question before the list of websites
- **AI Mode** is a conversational way of searching. People ask longer questions, follow up, compare options and refine, more like a chat than a single search

Both change how people search. Queries become longer and more conversational, and a single session can move from research to comparison to buying in one conversation.

## Where ads appear now

Ads, labelled "Sponsored", can now show in three kinds of place:

1. **Above and below an AI Overview,** in the familiar positions on the results page
2. **Inside an AI Overview,** within the AI-written answer itself, when the search has commercial intent
3. **Inside AI Mode,** alongside the conversational answer, including on follow-up questions

Google's help page on [ads in AI-powered experiences](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16297775) confirms that text ads and Shopping ads can appear within both AI Overviews and AI Mode. Availability inside the answers is still expanding by country and language, while ads above and below AI Overviews run much more widely.

## Which campaigns qualify

You do not need a new campaign or a separate budget. Existing campaigns take part automatically:

- **Search campaigns** are eligible. To show **inside** AI Overviews and AI Mode, they need broad match keywords or AI Max for Search
- **Shopping campaigns** are eligible, which makes the quality of your product feed more important than ever
- **Performance Max campaigns** are eligible

Search campaigns that use only exact and phrase match can still appear above and below AI Overviews, but not inside the AI answer. That is the single most important thing to check in your account.

### Do you need AI Max for Search?

No. Broad match Search campaigns are eligible on their own. AI Max for Search widens eligibility further by applying broad-match-style matching to your exact and phrase keywords, and Google is building its newest AI Mode features around AI Max and Performance Max. It is worth testing, not a requirement.

## What stays the same

- **The auction.** Ads in AI Overviews and AI Mode use the same auction and bidding as normal Search ads, with no extra charge to take part
- **Your guardrails.** Negative keywords and asset pinning still apply
- **Your account structure.** No new campaign type, budget or setting

## What you cannot control

- **You cannot target only AI placements,** and you cannot opt out of them separately from Search
- **You cannot see them separately in reporting.** Searches that trigger ads in AI answers are included in your normal campaign and search terms reports, not broken out on their own
- **Paying for ads does not affect organic AI answers.** Google states that buying ads does not influence which websites the AI cites, and being cited does not affect your ad eligibility

Because you cannot isolate these placements, judge them through your overall results: cost per lead or sale, and the quality of what comes in.

## What to change in your account

### 1. Check your match types

If every keyword is exact or phrase match, your ads cannot appear inside AI answers. If your conversion tracking is reliable and your conversions arrive every week, test broad match in one campaign, or test AI Max for Search, with an [experiment](/learn/google-ads-experiments-ab-testing-guide/) so you can compare against your current setup. [Google Ads match types explained](/learn/google-ads-keyword-match-types-explained/) covers what broad match actually reaches.

### 2. Strengthen your negative keywords

Broader matching brings in more varied searches, and conversational queries add many new combinations. Negative keywords still apply in AI placements, so keep them current. The [negative keyword generator](/tools/negative-keyword-generator/) helps build the list, and [how to find negative keywords](/learn/how-to-find-negative-keywords/) explains where they come from.

### 3. Read your search terms differently

Expect longer, question-style searches. Review the search terms report regularly and look for patterns rather than individual lines. The [search terms n-gram analyser](/tools/n-gram-analyser/) groups them by repeated words, which makes conversational queries much easier to judge.

### 4. Fix conversion tracking before widening reach

Broad match and AI Max decide which searches to enter based on your conversion data. If that data counts page views or duplicates, automation learns the wrong lesson faster. Make sure every conversion is a real lead or sale, and consider [enhanced conversions for leads](/learn/google-ads-enhanced-conversions-for-leads-guide/) if sales close offline.

### 5. Improve the inputs AI uses

AI placements match your ads against the context of the answer, not just the keyword. Clear, specific headlines, complete assets, and landing pages that answer the question directly all help. For Shopping and Performance Max, the product feed does most of this work: see [Google Shopping feed optimisation](/learn/google-shopping-feed-optimisation/).

### 6. Judge on business results

Since AI placements are blended into your reports, compare periods on what matters: enquiries, sales, cost per result and lead quality. A rise in clicks from broader matching is only good news if the cost per real customer holds.

## What it means beyond ads

AI answers also change organic search. When an AI Overview fully answers a simple question, some people never click through to a website. That makes paid visibility on commercial searches more valuable for many businesses, and it means your website should give clear, direct answers that AI systems can understand and cite. Ads and organic citations are separate systems, so one does not buy the other.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I opt out of ads in AI Overviews and AI Mode?

No. They are part of Google Search, so you cannot opt out of them separately or target them on their own. If your campaigns run on Search, Shopping or Performance Max, they may appear there when relevant.

### Do I need AI Max to show ads in AI Overviews?

No. Search campaigns with broad match keywords are eligible, as are Shopping and Performance Max campaigns. AI Max for Search widens eligibility further, but it is not required.

### Do ads in AI Overviews cost more?

They use the same auction and bidding as normal Search ads, and there is no extra charge to take part. What you pay still depends on competition, your bids and your ad quality.

### Can I see how my ads perform inside AI Overviews?

Not separately. Searches that trigger ads in AI Overviews and AI Mode are included in your normal campaign and search terms reports, so judge them through your overall results.

### Will running Google Ads help my website appear in AI answers?

No. Google keeps ads and organic AI answers separate. Buying ads does not influence which websites the AI cites, and being cited organically does not affect your ads.
