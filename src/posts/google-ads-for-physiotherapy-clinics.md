Google Ads works for physiotherapy clinics when it reaches people with a specific problem who are ready to book, and turns them into an initial assessment. Build campaigns around services and common reasons people search, such as back pain, sports injuries and post-surgery rehabilitation.

Make booking easy, describe treatment honestly, and judge campaigns on assessments booked and courses of treatment, not clicks.

For the wider approach across every kind of clinic, see [Google Ads for clinics](/google-ads-for-clinics/).

## How people search for a physio

Physiotherapy searches usually fall into three groups:

- **Direct service searches:** "physio near me", "sports physiotherapist in [town]". The clearest intent and the core of the budget
- **Problem searches:** back pain, knee pain, frozen shoulder, running injuries. People who know something is wrong and want help. Often valuable, but some only want advice
- **Specialist services:** post-surgery rehab, women's health physiotherapy, paediatric physio, home visits. Smaller volume, higher intent, often less competition

Give each group its own ad groups and landing pages. Someone searching for knee pain treatment wants to read about knees, not a list of every service.

## Structure by service and urgency

- **Pain and injury:** many people want an appointment within days, so availability matters in the ad
- **Sports and performance:** often booked around training, and responsive to specialist expertise
- **Rehabilitation:** referred or planned after surgery, with a longer course of treatment
- **Packages and memberships:** where you offer them, these can be advertised separately as ongoing care

Keep the location tight for most campaigns. People rarely travel far for a weekly appointment, though specialist services can draw from further away.

## Describe treatment honestly

Physiotherapy ads fall under Google's healthcare policies and your country's advertising rules:

- **Do not promise outcomes,** such as pain gone in one session or guaranteed recovery
- **Describe what happens:** an assessment, a diagnosis, a treatment plan
- **Check professional rules** for titles, testimonials and claims where you practise
- **Be careful with personalised advertising.** Google restricts targeting based on health conditions, so some remarketing and audience options may not be allowed

Honest, specific wording usually performs better anyway. "Book an assessment with a sports physiotherapist this week" says more than any promise.

## Build landing pages for booking

A physio landing page should let someone book in under a minute:

- The problem or service in the headline, matching the search
- Next available appointment times, if your system shows them
- Online booking at the top, with the phone number beside it
- What to expect at the first appointment and how long it takes
- Prices, or how insurance and self-pay work where relevant
- The physiotherapists' qualifications, stated accurately

## Measure assessments and courses of treatment

A first assessment is the start, not the end. Track:

1. **Initial assessments booked** from ads
2. **Attendance,** because no-shows are common for first appointments
3. **Follow-up sessions,** which show the real value of a new patient

Record the source of each new patient in your booking or practice software, and compare cost per new patient with what a course of treatment is worth. [Enhanced conversions for leads](/learn/google-ads-enhanced-conversions-for-leads-guide/) explains how to send real outcomes back to Google Ads.

## Where physio budgets leak

- Exercise videos, stretches and home treatment searches
- Physiotherapy degree, course and job searches
- Free public services, where you do not provide them
- Equipment and product searches, such as braces and foam rollers
- Searches from areas too far away for regular appointments

## Frequently asked questions

### Do Google Ads work for physiotherapy clinics?

Yes. People search for a physio when they have pain or an injury and want help soon. Campaigns built around services and common problems, with easy booking and honest wording, usually bring a steady flow of new assessments.

### Should physio ads target pain and injury searches?

Yes, with care. Searches such as "knee pain physio" often show strong intent, but some people only want advice or exercises. Use specific keywords, add negatives for home exercise searches, and measure bookings rather than clicks.

### Can physiotherapy clinics use remarketing?

With care. Google restricts personalised advertising based on health conditions, so remarketing lists built around specific conditions may not be allowed. Check the current policy and what your account permits.

### What should a physio clinic track in Google Ads?

Initial assessments booked, attended appointments and follow-up sessions. Cost per new patient who completes a course of treatment is a better measure than cost per click or call.

### How far should a physio clinic target?

Usually a small area, because patients come weekly or more often. Specialist services, such as post-surgery rehab or women's health physiotherapy, can justify a wider radius.
