Google Ads works for hearing clinics when it reaches two audiences: people who think they may have hearing loss, and the family members who often search on their behalf. Lead with a hearing test as the first step, be clear about hearing aid prices and options, and measure tests that turn into fittings, not just appointments booked.

For the wider approach across every kind of clinic, see [Google Ads for clinics](/google-ads-for-clinics/).

## Who searches for hearing care

- **The person with hearing loss:** "hearing test near me", "hearing aids", "ringing in ears". Often older, often searching after months of putting it off
- **Family members:** sons, daughters and partners searching for a parent or spouse, comparing clinics and prices
- **Existing hearing aid users:** repairs, batteries, upgrades and new fittings

Ads and landing pages should speak to both the patient and the person helping them. A clear, calm page that a family member can share works better than a hard sell.

## Organise campaigns by need

- **Hearing tests:** the main entry point, local and steady. Show availability and how long the test takes
- **Hearing aids:** higher value, more comparison, and competition from retailers and online sellers
- **Tinnitus and specialist services:** smaller volume, specific intent, often worth a separate campaign
- **Repairs and aftercare:** valuable for retaining existing patients and winning those whose original provider closed or moved

## Hearing tests as the first step

Most hearing aid fittings start with a test. Make booking a test easy:

- Free or low-cost tests, if you offer them, stated clearly with any conditions
- What happens during the test and how long it takes
- Home visits, if you offer them, for people with limited mobility
- Online booking and a phone number at the top, because many patients prefer to call

## Be clear about hearing aid prices

Price is one of the first questions, and many people expect it to be hidden. What tends to build trust:

- Price ranges or starting points for different levels of hearing aid
- What is included: fitting, follow-up appointments, aftercare and warranty
- Finance or payment plans if offered, including that approval may be required
- How you compare with buying online, explained honestly: assessment, fitting and adjustments

## Health advertising rules

Hearing care ads follow Google's healthcare policies. Avoid promising that a hearing aid will restore normal hearing, describe products and services accurately, and check your country's rules on medical devices and professional titles. Google also restricts personalised advertising based on health conditions, which limits remarketing. [What clinics can and cannot advertise on Google Ads](/learn/healthcare-google-ads-policy-for-clinics/) covers the rules in more detail.

## Measure tests that become fittings

Track:

1. **Hearing tests booked** and attended
2. **Fittings,** with the value of the hearing aids sold
3. **Aftercare and repair appointments,** which show long-term value

A campaign with a low cost per test but few fittings may be attracting the wrong searches. Record the source of each patient in your practice system and compare cost per fitting with its value. [How to track new patients from Google Ads](/learn/dental-google-ads-conversion-tracking/) describes a method that works the same way for hearing clinics.

## Where hearing clinic budgets leak

- Cheap hearing aid and online retailer searches, unless you sell online
- Batteries and accessories searches, unless you want that business
- Audiology courses, jobs and training
- Free public services, where you do not provide them
- Areas too far away for regular appointments

## Frequently asked questions

### Do Google Ads work for hearing clinics?

Yes. People search for hearing tests and hearing aids when they are ready to act, and family members often search for them. Campaigns that lead with a hearing test and track fittings usually work well.

### Should hearing clinics show hearing aid prices?

Giving a starting price or range on the landing page usually builds trust, because many people expect prices to be hidden. Explain what is included, such as fitting and aftercare, so the comparison with online sellers is fair.

### Should hearing clinic ads target family members?

Yes, through the wording and landing pages rather than audience targeting. Clear information that a son, daughter or partner can read and share helps them encourage someone to book a test.

### Can hearing clinics use remarketing?

With care. Google restricts personalised advertising based on health conditions, so remarketing based on hearing loss content may not be allowed. Check the current policy for your services.

### What should a hearing clinic track in Google Ads?

Hearing tests booked and attended, fittings with their value, and aftercare appointments. Cost per fitting shows profitability better than cost per test.
