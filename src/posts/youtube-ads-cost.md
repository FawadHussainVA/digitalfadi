YouTube ads cost depends on how you pay and what you ask Google to optimise for. Some formats charge per view, some per thousand impressions, and campaigns built for leads or sales bid towards a cost per conversion. You set your own budget, so there is no fixed price.

The useful question is not what a view costs, but what a customer from YouTube costs compared with what they are worth.

This guide explains the pricing models behind YouTube ads cost, what pushes prices up or down, and how to budget.

## The pricing models

**Cost per view (CPV).** Common for skippable in-stream and in-feed video ads. You generally pay when someone watches a set length of your ad, or the whole ad if it is shorter, or interacts with it. People who skip early cost nothing.

**Cost per thousand impressions (CPM).** Used for formats built for reach, such as short non-skippable and bumper ads, and campaigns aimed at reaching as many people as possible. You pay for every thousand times the ad is shown.

**Conversion bidding.** Campaigns built for leads or sales, such as Demand Gen, bid towards a target cost per action or return on ad spend. You still pay per click or impression, but Google optimises towards conversions. [Demand Gen campaigns explained](/learn/demand-gen-campaigns/) covers how these work.

## What pushes YouTube ads cost up or down

- **Audience:** narrow, high-value audiences cost more than broad ones
- **Format:** reach formats, view formats and conversion campaigns are priced differently
- **Season:** costs often rise when many advertisers compete, such as the run-up to the holidays
- **Creative quality:** ads people watch and engage with tend to perform better in the auction
- **Bidding goal:** asking for conversions usually costs more per view than asking for views, but can cost less per customer
- **Location:** some countries and cities are more competitive than others

## How to set a YouTube ads budget

1. **Decide the goal.** Awareness, consideration or direct leads and sales each need a different campaign and a different measure
2. **Work backwards from value.** For lead or sales campaigns, decide what you can pay for a customer and budget for enough conversions to learn from
3. **Allow time.** Video campaigns often influence people who convert later through search, so judge results over several weeks
4. **Start with one campaign** and one or two strong videos rather than spreading a small budget thin

## How to measure YouTube ads properly

Views and view rate show whether people watch. They do not show whether the business grows. Track:

- **Conversions** from YouTube campaigns, including view-through conversions where relevant
- **Brand searches,** which often rise when video reaches the right people
- **Cost per lead or sale** across the account, not just in the YouTube campaign

## Getting more for the same YouTube ads cost

- Put the message in the first few seconds, before people can skip
- Make versions for different placements, including vertical video for Shorts
- Exclude placements and audiences that waste budget
- Use your customer lists and website visitors where your account and policies allow

## Frequently asked questions

### How much do YouTube ads cost?

There is no fixed price. You set your own budget, and you pay per view, per thousand impressions or towards a conversion goal, depending on the format and campaign. Competition, audience and creative quality all affect the final cost.

### Do I pay for skipped YouTube ads?

Generally not for skippable formats charged per view. You usually pay when someone watches a set length of the ad, the whole ad if it is shorter, or interacts with it.

### What is a good YouTube ads budget for a small business?

Enough to reach your audience regularly and, for lead or sales campaigns, enough to produce conversions to learn from. Start with one campaign and one or two strong videos rather than spreading a small budget thin.

### Are YouTube ads cheaper than Search ads?

Per view, often yes. Per customer, it depends. Search reaches people already looking for you, while YouTube reaches people earlier in their decision, so compare cost per lead or sale rather than click or view prices.

### How do I know if YouTube ads are working?

Track conversions, brand search growth and cost per lead or sale across the account over several weeks. View counts alone do not show whether YouTube is profitable.
