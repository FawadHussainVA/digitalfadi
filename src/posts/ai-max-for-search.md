AI Max for Search is a set of automation features inside Google Search campaigns. It can show your ads on searches beyond your keyword list, write extra ad text from your website, and send people to the page on your site it judges most relevant.

It is now switched on by default for new Search campaigns, so every advertiser needs to decide whether AI Max for Search helps their account or wastes budget.

This guide explains each part of AI Max for Search, the controls you keep, and when it is worth using.

## The three parts of AI Max for Search

**1. Search term matching.** Your ads can show for searches that go beyond your keywords, using broad match and keywordless technology based on your ads and landing pages. In the search terms report, these appear with "AI Max" as the match type, and a source column shows whether the match came from broad match expansion or keywordless matching.

**2. Text customization.** Formerly called automatically created assets. Google generates headlines and descriptions from your landing page, existing ads and keywords. You can remove individual generated assets.

**3. Final URL expansion.** Google can send people to a different page on your domain if it is more relevant to the search, and adjust the ad text to match. It requires text customization to be on.

## The controls you keep

- **Turn parts off individually.** Text customization and Final URL expansion can each be switched off in campaign settings. Turning off text customization also turns off Final URL expansion
- **Brand exclusions and inclusions** at campaign level
- **URL exclusions** to keep pages such as careers, blog posts or checkout out of Final URL expansion
- **URL inclusions** to add pages Google did not pick up
- **Negative keywords** still apply to AI Max searches
- **Reporting:** the AI Max match type, landing page and asset reports show what AI Max for Search actually did

## The risks to watch

- **Pinned headlines are not guaranteed.** With Final URL expansion or URL inclusions on, pinned responsive search ad assets are not respected. If a headline must always show, keep Final URL expansion off
- **Unexpected landing pages.** Traffic can reach pages you never intended for ads unless you exclude them
- **Broader, less relevant searches.** Search term matching can waste budget on loosely related queries if negatives are not maintained
- **Wording rules.** Generated text may not suit regulated industries such as legal, healthcare or finance
- **Tracking templates.** If you use one, test that dynamic landing pages still track correctly

## When AI Max for Search makes sense

**Good fit:**
- Accounts with reliable conversion tracking and enough conversions for Smart Bidding. AI Max features need conversion-based Smart Bidding to work as intended
- Websites where most pages are good landing pages, such as large e-commerce catalogues
- Advertisers who review the search terms report every week or two

**Poor fit:**
- Accounts with few conversions or unreliable tracking
- Regulated industries where every word in an ad must be approved
- Sites with many pages that should never receive paid traffic

## How to test AI Max for Search safely

1. Fix conversion tracking first, so the automation learns from real leads or sales
2. Add URL exclusions and refresh negative keywords before switching it on
3. Run it as an [experiment](/learn/google-ads-experiments-ab-testing-guide/) against your current setup rather than switching the whole campaign
4. Judge results on cost per lead or sale, not clicks, over several weeks
5. Review the AI Max rows in the search terms report and add negatives for irrelevant searches

## Frequently asked questions

### What is AI Max for Search?

AI Max for Search is a set of automation features in Google Search campaigns: search term matching beyond your keywords, text customization that generates ad text, and Final URL expansion that can send people to other relevant pages on your site.

### Is AI Max for Search on by default?

Yes, for new Search campaigns. Existing campaigns keep their settings unless you change them, so check new campaigns before launching.

### Can I use AI Max but keep my landing pages?

Yes. Keep search term matching and text customization on but switch off Final URL expansion. Your ads will then use the final URLs you set.

### Does AI Max for Search respect negative keywords?

Yes. Negative keywords still apply, which is why reviewing the search terms report and adding negatives matters more when AI Max is on.

### How do I see which searches came from AI Max?

In the search terms report, AI Max searches show "AI Max" as the match type, with a source column explaining whether the match came from broad match expansion or keywordless matching.
