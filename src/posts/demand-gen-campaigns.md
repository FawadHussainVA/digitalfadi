Demand Gen campaigns show image and video ads to people who are not yet searching for you, on YouTube, Discover, Gmail and Google's display network. They create interest rather than capture it.

Use Demand Gen campaigns when Search is already working and you want to reach more of the right people earlier, and judge them over a longer window than Search, because many of the customers they influence convert later.

## Where Demand Gen campaigns show

- **YouTube:** in-stream, in-feed and Shorts
- **Discover:** the feed of articles and content in the Google app
- **Gmail:** the Promotions and Social tabs
- **Google's display network:** websites and apps that show Google ads

Channel controls let you choose which of these placements a campaign uses, so you can, for example, run on YouTube only.

## Formats

- **Single image ads**
- **Video ads,** including vertical video for Shorts
- **Carousel ads** with several images
- **Product ads** from a Merchant Center feed, for retailers

Strong creative matters more in Demand Gen campaigns than in Search, because people are scrolling or watching, not looking for you.

## Audiences

- **Your data:** customer lists and website visitors, where your account and Google's policies allow
- **Lookalike segments** based on your own lists
- **Custom segments** built from search terms, websites or apps your customers use
- **Interests and life events**

Start with your own data and lookalikes, because they usually perform best.

## Bidding

Demand Gen campaigns can bid for clicks, conversions, a target cost per action, conversion value or a target return on ad spend. Conversion bidding needs reliable conversion tracking and enough conversions to learn from. [Which Google Ads bid strategy should you use?](/learn/which-google-ads-bid-strategy-should-you-use/) explains the trade-offs.

## How Demand Gen differs from Search and Performance Max

- **Search** reaches people actively searching. It captures demand
- **Demand Gen campaigns** reach people before they search. They create demand, with more control over creative, audiences and placements
- **Performance Max** spreads one campaign across all Google channels with less visibility into where ads show. [Performance Max vs Search campaigns](/learn/performance-max-vs-search-campaigns/) compares those two

## When to use Demand Gen campaigns

**Good fit:**
- Search is profitable and you want more of the right customers
- You have strong images or video
- Your product benefits from being seen before people search, such as new products, considered purchases or local services with competitors

**Not yet:**
- Search is not profitable or tracking is unreliable
- You have no creative beyond text ads
- The budget is too small to give two campaign types enough data

## How to measure Demand Gen campaigns

- Track conversions, including view-through conversions where relevant
- Watch brand searches and direct traffic, which often rise when Demand Gen reaches the right people
- Compare cost per lead or sale across the whole account before and after, not just inside the campaign
- Allow several weeks before judging

## Frequently asked questions

### What are Demand Gen campaigns?

Demand Gen campaigns are Google Ads campaigns that show image and video ads on YouTube, Discover, Gmail and Google's display network to people who are not yet searching for you, using audiences such as your customer data and lookalike segments.

### Are Demand Gen campaigns good for lead generation?

They can be, if conversion tracking is reliable and the creative is strong. They usually cost more per lead than Search at first, but can bring people who would not have found you otherwise.

### What is the difference between Demand Gen and Performance Max?

Demand Gen focuses on visual placements and gives more control over creative, audiences and channels. Performance Max runs across all Google channels, including Search, with less control and visibility.

### Can I run Demand Gen only on YouTube?

Yes. Channel controls let you choose which placements a Demand Gen campaign uses, including YouTube only.

### How long should I test Demand Gen campaigns?

Several weeks at least, because many people they reach convert later through search or direct visits. Judge them on account-wide results, not clicks.
