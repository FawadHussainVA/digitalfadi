Weight management clinics can advertise on Google, but they face some of the strictest rules of any clinic. Prescription weight loss medicine names are restricted in ads, keywords and landing pages in most countries, unrealistic claims and misleading images get ads disapproved, and Google limits personalised advertising around health.

Campaigns that work advertise the consultation and the programme, not the medicine, make realistic promises, and measure patients who start and stay on a programme. For the wider approach across every kind of clinic, see [Google Ads for clinics](/google-ads-for-clinics/).

## The rules come first

### Medicine names

Many weight loss treatments involve prescription medicines. In most countries, Google does not allow prescription drug terms in ad text, keywords or landing pages without certification, and online prescribing services need certification and are only allowed in certain countries. In practice:

- Advertise a **medically supervised weight loss consultation** or **programme**, not a named medicine
- Check landing pages as well as ads, because a medicine name on the page can disapprove the ad
- If you prescribe online, check whether your service needs Google certification before building campaigns

### Claims and images

- **No unrealistic promises,** such as a specific amount of weight in a short time, or results without effort
- **No guaranteed outcomes.** Results differ from person to person
- **Before-and-after images** that suggest unlikely results can be disapproved, and some regulators restrict them entirely
- **Body-focused imagery** can also trigger other ad policies, so keep images respectful and relevant

[What clinics can and cannot advertise on Google Ads](/learn/healthcare-google-ads-policy-for-clinics/) explains these rules in more detail.

## Searches to target

- **Clinic searches:** "weight loss clinic near me", "medical weight loss in [city]"
- **Programme searches:** supervised weight loss, nutrition programmes, weight management for specific health needs
- **Consultation and cost searches:** people comparing options and prices

Keep keywords on the clinic and programme. Searches for a medicine by name may not be allowed, and they attract people looking to buy it, not to join a programme.

## Write ads that build trust

- Lead with supervision and support: who runs the programme and their qualifications
- Explain the process: consultation, assessment, plan and follow-up
- Be clear about cost, including monthly fees and what is included
- Use calm, respectful language. Shame-based or pressure wording performs badly and can break policies

## Landing pages

- What the programme involves, step by step
- Who it is suitable for, and that suitability is decided at a consultation
- Costs and what is included
- The clinicians, with qualifications stated accurately
- Booking and a phone number at the top, and a short form that asks only for what is needed

## Privacy and remarketing

Weight and health information is sensitive. Google restricts personalised advertising based on health, so remarketing for weight loss services may be limited. Keep booking forms short, never ask for detailed health information on an ad landing page, and agree tracking on booking pages with whoever handles compliance.

## Measure programme starts and retention

Track:

1. **Consultations booked and attended**
2. **Programme starts,** with their value
3. **Patients still on the programme** after the first months, where your system can show it

A campaign that brings many consultations but few programme starts may be attracting people looking for a quick fix or a medicine, not your service.

## Where weight management budgets leak

- Medicine names, supplements and diet product searches
- Free diet plans, calorie calculators and recipe searches
- Gym, fitness class and personal trainer searches, unless you offer them
- Nutrition courses and jobs
- Public services you do not provide

## Frequently asked questions

### Can weight loss clinics advertise on Google Ads?

Yes, within strict rules. Advertise the consultation and programme, avoid prescription medicine names, make realistic claims, and check whether your services need certification, especially if you prescribe online.

### Can I use weight loss medicine names in Google Ads?

In most countries, no. Prescription drug terms are restricted in ads, keywords and landing pages without certification. Advertise a medically supervised consultation or programme instead.

### Are before-and-after photos allowed in weight loss ads?

Often not, or only in limited ways. Images that suggest unlikely results can be disapproved, and some regulators restrict them entirely. Check Google's policies and your local rules before using them.

### Can weight management clinics use remarketing?

Usually only in limited ways, because Google restricts personalised advertising based on health. Search campaigns, which respond to what someone types, are generally unaffected.

### What should a weight management clinic track in Google Ads?

Consultations booked and attended, programme starts and their value, and retention where possible. Cost per programme start is a better measure than cost per enquiry.
