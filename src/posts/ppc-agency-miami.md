## What makes Miami different for Google Ads

- **Bilingual by default.** A large share of Miami customers search in Spanish, and many switch between languages. Separate Spanish ads and landing pages often reach customers competitors miss. [The Google Ads language setting update](/learn/google-ads-language-setting-update/) explains how Google now matches ad language
- **Tourism and seasons.** Winter brings visitors and seasonal residents, which lifts demand for hospitality, dining, rentals, boat and event services. Budgets should follow those peaks
- **Hurricane season.** Storms create sudden demand for restoration, roofing, generators, windows and shutters, and pause many other services
- **International clients.** Medical, legal, real estate and luxury services often attract clients from Latin America, which affects targeting and language
- **Competitive niches.** Legal, aesthetics, real estate and home services are heavily contested, so tracking and structure decide profitability

## What good PPC management in Miami looks like

1. **Track real results:** calls, forms, bookings and sales, matched to your own records
2. **Run English and Spanish campaigns** where your team can serve both, with matching landing pages
3. **Target real service areas** across Miami-Dade, Broward and beyond, using people in the area rather than interest. [The location setting that wastes budget](/learn/google-ads-location-targeting-settings/) explains the difference
4. **Plan for the seasons,** raising budgets for winter peaks and keeping emergency campaigns ready for storms
5. **Report clearly** on leads or sales and their cost

## Spanish campaigns: do it properly

- Write ads in natural Spanish, not machine-translated English
- Send Spanish ads to Spanish landing pages
- Make sure calls and forms in Spanish are answered in Spanish
- Measure Spanish and English campaigns separately, because conversion rates often differ

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I hire a PPC agency in Miami or a freelancer?

Either can work. An agency offers a team; a freelancer means you deal directly with the person doing the work, usually for a lower fee. Check who will run the account, how results are tracked, and that the account stays in your name.

### Should Miami businesses run Google Ads in Spanish?

Often, yes. Many Miami customers search in Spanish. Use natural Spanish ads, Spanish landing pages, and make sure enquiries are answered in Spanish.

### How does hurricane season affect Google Ads in Miami?

It creates demand spikes for restoration, roofing and storm services, and lowers demand for others. Keep emergency campaigns ready, and pause or reduce campaigns for services you cannot deliver during storms.

### How much does a PPC agency in Miami cost?

You pay Google for ads, plus a management fee, usually monthly or a percentage of spend. Judge it on the cost per customer it produces.

### Can I target Spanish speakers in Miami with Google Ads?

Google now matches Search ads to the language people search in, rather than a campaign language setting. Writing ads and landing pages in natural Spanish is the main way to reach Spanish-speaking customers.
