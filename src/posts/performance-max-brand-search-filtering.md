Google has updated its help documentation to say that Performance Max campaigns may filter out navigational and brand-specific searches, so that those searches are served by a dedicated brand Search campaign instead. In practice, if you run a brand Search campaign alongside Performance Max, more of your brand traffic should now go through Search, where you control the keywords, ads and budget. The risk is that a brand campaign with too little budget or weak ads could now miss searches that Performance Max used to pick up. Check your brand campaign's budget and ads this week, and expect Performance Max's reported results to drop slightly if it was collecting brand conversions before.

## What Google changed

The change was reported in early November 2026. It appears in Google's help page on how Performance Max interacts with other campaigns in your account. A new paragraph says Performance Max may filter out navigational or brand-specific queries so they can be handled by dedicated Search campaigns, which Google describes as offering more precise control. The [Search Engine Roundtable report](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ads-pmax-brand-queries-42438.html) shows the new wording next to the earlier version of the page.

The same paragraph gives two pieces of advice: make sure your brand Search campaigns have enough budget and good, eligible ads, and, if you want Performance Max to capture this traffic, consider adding search themes to it.

Note the word "may". Google has not said exactly when the filtering applies or how much traffic it affects, so the only reliable way to know what is happening in your account is to look.

## Why it matters

Brand searches are the cheapest and easiest conversions in most accounts. People searching your business name were usually going to find you anyway.

When Performance Max is allowed to serve on those searches, it reports those easy conversions as its own, and its numbers look better than the work it is really doing. That is why brand exclusions in Performance Max have been standard advice for a long time, and why [Performance Max: what it hides](/learn/performance-max-what-it-hides/) starts with brand.

This change moves Google's default behaviour in the same direction. It is broadly good news for advertisers who want honest reporting. It only becomes a problem if the Search campaign that is supposed to catch the brand traffic is not ready for it.

## Five checks to run now

**1. Confirm you have a brand Search campaign.** A Search campaign targeting your business name and close variants, kept separate from your other campaigns. If you have none, decide first whether you should bid on your brand at all. [Should you bid on your own brand name?](/learn/should-you-bid-on-your-own-brand-name/) walks through that decision.

**2. Check its budget.** If the brand campaign shows "Limited by budget", it may now miss searches that Performance Max used to catch. Look at its search impression share and the share lost to budget. Brand campaigns usually need little money, so this is often a quick fix.

**3. Check its ads are eligible.** Disapproved or limited ads, missing sitelinks or an outdated offer will all cost you clicks on the searches that matter most. Make sure at least one responsive search ad is approved and showing.

**4. Re-baseline Performance Max.** If Performance Max was picking up brand conversions before, its conversion numbers may fall and its cost per conversion may rise over the coming weeks. That is not necessarily a drop in performance. It may simply be the brand conversions moving to where they belong. Compare total conversions across the account, not campaign by campaign, before deciding anything.

**5. Keep your brand exclusions.** "May filter" is not a guarantee. If you already exclude your brand from Performance Max, leave the exclusion in place. It is an explicit control, and you should not rely on an automatic one Google can change without notice.

## Should you add brand search themes to Performance Max?

Usually not. Google suggests it for advertisers who want Performance Max to capture brand traffic, but for most businesses that brings back the reporting problem the change helps to solve. Keep brand in Search, where you can see the exact searches and set the budget, and judge Performance Max on new customers it finds.

There is one exception. If you do not run any brand Search campaign and do not want to, and your brand traffic is being taken by competitors bidding on your name, test a dedicated brand Search campaign first. It gives you clearer data than search themes inside Performance Max.

## How to see what is happening in your account

In the Performance Max campaign, open the search terms insights and look for categories that contain your business name. Compare a few weeks before the change with a few weeks after. In your brand Search campaign, watch impressions and impression share over the same period.

If brand searches drop out of Performance Max and do not appear in Search, your brand campaign is missing them, and checks 2 and 3 above are the place to start. The guide to [running Performance Max and Search together](/learn/performance-max-vs-search-campaigns/) covers how to keep the two from competing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will Performance Max stop showing ads on my brand name completely?

Not necessarily. Google's wording is that Performance Max may filter these searches to prioritise dedicated Search campaigns. It has not said this happens in every case, so check your own search terms insights rather than assuming.

### What if I do not have a brand Search campaign?

Google's guidance is written for accounts that do. If you have none, look at whether your brand searches are still being served and by which campaign. Whether you need a brand campaign at all depends on whether competitors bid on your name and whether you rank first organically.

### Why did my Performance Max results get worse this month?

One possible reason is that brand conversions, which are cheap and easy, are now going to your Search campaign instead. Check whether total conversions across the account have held steady before changing Performance Max budgets or targets.

### Should I remove my brand exclusions now?

No. Keep them. An exclusion you set is a firm instruction, while the new filtering is something Google says may happen and can change at any time.

### How do I know if my brand campaign has enough budget?

Check whether it shows as "Limited by budget" and how much search impression share it loses to budget. On brand searches, you should aim to show for nearly every search that is genuinely about your business.
