Google Ads lead form assets let people send you their details directly from your ad, without visiting your website. They can show on Search, YouTube and other Google placements. Lead forms reduce friction and can raise lead volume, but they also make it easy to submit low-quality enquiries, so use qualifying questions, connect leads to your CRM, and follow up fast.

This guide explains how Google Ads lead form assets work, how to set them up, and how to keep lead quality high.

## How Google Ads lead form assets work

A lead form is an asset attached to a campaign. When someone taps it, a form opens inside Google, often pre-filled with their Google account details such as name, email or phone. They submit, and the lead goes to you. Form submissions are counted as conversions in Google Ads.

Lead forms can appear in Search, Performance Max, YouTube and Demand Gen campaigns, where your account is eligible. Google limits them in some sensitive categories and requires a privacy policy link on every form.

## Setting up a lead form

1. Add a lead form asset at campaign level
2. Write a clear headline and description: what the person gets by submitting
3. Choose the fields you need, such as name, email, phone and postcode
4. Add qualifying questions, for example the service needed, budget or timeframe
5. Add your privacy policy URL
6. Write a confirmation message with a next step, such as when you will call
7. Choose how to receive leads: download them, or connect a CRM or webhook

## Get leads to the right place, fast

Leads sitting in Google Ads are easy to miss. Connect lead forms to your CRM or email through a webhook or integration so each lead reaches your team within minutes. Download any leads you have not sent elsewhere regularly, because Google keeps lead form data only for a limited time.

Speed matters: people who submit a lead form often contacted competitors too. [Why your Google Ads calls are not becoming jobs](/learn/why-google-ads-calls-are-not-becoming-jobs/) covers the follow-up side.

## Keep lead quality high

- **Choose the more qualified option** where offered, which adds a review step before submitting
- **Ask one or two qualifying questions,** such as service or timeframe
- **Avoid vague promises** such as "free quote" with no detail, which attract casual submissions
- **Track real outcomes,** sending qualified leads and sales back to Google Ads, so bidding learns which leads matter. [Enhanced conversions for leads](/learn/google-ads-enhanced-conversions-for-leads-guide/) explains how

## Lead forms vs landing pages

**Lead forms often win when:**
- Your audience is mostly on mobile
- Your website is slow or hard to use on phones
- The offer is simple, such as a callback or a quote

**Landing pages often win when:**
- People need information, prices or proof before contacting you
- You sell higher-value services where quality matters more than volume
- You want to test page content and messaging

Many accounts use both, and compare cost per qualified lead rather than cost per form.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are Google Ads lead form assets?

Lead form assets let people submit their contact details directly from your Google ad, in a form hosted by Google, without visiting your website.

### Where do Google Ads lead forms show?

They can appear in Search, Performance Max, YouTube and Demand Gen campaigns, where your account and industry are eligible.

### How do I get leads from Google Ads lead forms?

You can download them from Google Ads or connect a CRM or webhook so leads arrive automatically. Download or sync regularly, because Google keeps the data only for a limited time.

### Are Google Ads lead form leads lower quality?

They can be, because submitting is so easy. Qualifying questions, the more qualified form option and fast follow-up help keep quality high.

### Do lead form submissions count as conversions?

Yes. Lead form submissions are counted as conversions in Google Ads. For better bidding, also send qualified leads and sales back to Google Ads.
