You probably need a Google Ads expert if you are spending money without knowing which ads bring customers, if leads have dropped and you cannot say why, or if the account takes hours you do not have. The clearest sign is not trusting the numbers: when conversion tracking is unclear, every other decision is a guess.

Most of the signs below can be checked in an afternoon. If several apply, an outside review usually pays for itself quickly.

## 1. You spend money but cannot say what it produces

If you cannot answer "how many enquiries or sales came from Google Ads last month, and what did each cost?", the account is not being managed against results. Clicks and impressions are not the answer.

## 2. You do not trust the conversion numbers

Google Ads shows more conversions than your team saw, or far fewer. Calls of a few seconds count as leads, or a page view counts as a sale. Broken tracking misleads both you and automated bidding. [Why are my Google Ads not converting?](/learn/why-are-my-google-ads-not-converting/) covers the usual causes.

## 3. Results dropped and nobody knows why

Costs rose, leads fell, or both, and the account shows no clear reason. An expert checks the change history, auctions, search terms, tracking and landing pages before changing anything, so the real cause is found rather than guessed.

## 4. The search terms report surprises you

When you open the search terms report, you find job seekers, DIY searches, competitors' names or areas you do not serve. That money is wasted every day until negatives are added. [How to find negative keywords](/learn/how-to-find-negative-keywords/) explains the fix.

## 5. You switched on automation and lost control

Smart Bidding, broad match or Performance Max were turned on, results became harder to read, and you are not sure what the campaigns are doing. Automation works well with good data and guardrails, and badly without them. [What Performance Max hides](/learn/performance-max-what-it-hides/) shows where to look.

## 6. The account has not been touched in months

Google Ads changes constantly: new features, policy updates, competitors entering your auctions. An account that nobody reviews drifts, even if it once worked well.

## 7. Your agency's reports show activity, not results

Reports full of impressions and click-through rates, with no link to leads or sales, and no explanation of what changed last month. [How to tell if your Google Ads agency is doing anything](/learn/is-your-google-ads-agency-doing-anything/) lists what to check.

## 8. You do not own the account

If someone else controls the account, you cannot see what is happening, and leaving could mean losing the history. That is worth fixing before anything else.

## 9. You are about to spend more

Increasing the budget on an account with tracking or targeting problems multiplies the waste. A review before scaling up is cheaper than discovering the problem at twice the spend.

## 10. It takes time you do not have

Running an account properly means regular search term reviews, tests, budget changes and tracking checks. If it competes with running the business, it usually loses.

## What to do next

1. **Check tracking first.** Compare Google Ads conversions with the leads or sales you actually received last month
2. **Read the search terms report** for the last 30 days
3. **Get an outside view** before paying for ongoing management, so you know what needs fixing and how much work it is

## Frequently asked questions

### When should I hire a Google Ads expert?

When you cannot tell what your ad spend produces, do not trust the conversion numbers, see results drop without a clear reason, or do not have time to manage the account properly. Before increasing your budget is also a good time.

### Can I fix my Google Ads account myself?

Sometimes. Clear problems, such as missing negative keywords or the wrong location setting, can be fixed in-house. Tracking problems, automation issues and falling results usually need more experience to diagnose correctly.

### What is the most common problem in Google Ads accounts?

Conversion tracking that counts the wrong things, such as page views, short calls or duplicates. It is common because the account still appears to work, while bidding learns from the wrong data.

### How quickly can an expert improve my account?

Wasted spend from irrelevant searches can often be cut within days. Improvements that depend on better tracking and bidding usually take several weeks to show, because automated bidding needs time to learn from the new data.

### What should I ask a Google Ads expert first?

Ask what they would check first in your account. A good answer starts with tracking, then search terms and structure, rather than promising results before looking.
