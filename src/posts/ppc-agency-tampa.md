## What makes Tampa Bay different for Google Ads

- **A bay that splits the market.** Bridges separate Tampa, St. Petersburg and Clearwater, and many customers will not cross them for a routine service. Target each side of the bay deliberately rather than the whole region
- **Growth and new residents.** People moving to the area search for new providers: dentists, vets, home services and property managers
- **Seasonal residents.** Winter visitors lift demand for healthcare, home services, rentals and hospitality, then ease off in summer
- **Storm and heat seasons.** Hurricanes and heavy rain drive demand for roofing, restoration and tree services, while heat keeps HVAC and pest control busy
- **Older and retired customers** in parts of the region, which matters for healthcare, hearing, home care and financial services

## What good PPC management in Tampa looks like

1. **Track real results,** matching calls, forms and bookings with what your team received
2. **Target by area,** using cities or radius around where you work, set to people in your area. [The location setting that wastes budget](/learn/google-ads-location-targeting-settings/) explains the difference
3. **Plan budgets by season,** raising them for winter demand and storm periods, and easing off when demand drops
4. **Keep emergency campaigns ready** for storm-related services, paused until needed
5. **Review search terms** regularly and add negative keywords
6. **Report** leads or sales and their cost, with what changed and why

## Planning for seasonal residents

- Increase budgets for services seasonal residents use as winter approaches
- Write ads that speak to new or returning residents, such as availability for new patients or clients
- Track results by season, so you can compare this winter with last winter rather than with summer

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I hire a PPC agency in Tampa or a freelancer?

Either can work. An agency offers a team; a freelancer means you deal directly with the person doing the work, usually for a lower fee. Check who will run the account, how results are tracked, and that the account stays in your name.

### Should I target all of Tampa Bay with Google Ads?

Usually not. Many customers will not cross the bay for a routine service. Target the cities and areas you actually serve, and check the user locations report.

### How do seasonal residents affect Google Ads in Tampa?

They raise demand for healthcare, home services, rentals and hospitality in winter. Plan budgets for those months and compare results season to season.

### How much does a PPC agency in Tampa cost?

You pay Google directly for ads, plus a management fee, usually monthly or a percentage of spend. Judge the cost against the customers it produces.

### Should Tampa Bay businesses pause Google Ads during a hurricane?

Pause or reduce campaigns for services you cannot deliver, and switch on prepared emergency campaigns for services that are in demand, such as restoration or roofing, as long as you can answer calls and send crews.
