Google Ads works for hair transplant and hair loss clinics when campaigns separate surgical transplants from non-surgical treatment, lead with a consultation, and give patients a clear reason to choose you over cheaper clinics abroad. Patients compare prices across countries, read reviews for months and want to know exactly who will perform the procedure.

Follow the rules on medicine names and claims, and measure consultations that become booked procedures. For the wider approach across every kind of clinic, see [Google Ads for clinics](/google-ads-for-clinics/).

## Two kinds of patient

**Surgical: hair transplants.** FUE, FUT and related procedures. High value, long research, and strong price comparison with clinics abroad. The consultation is the key step.

**Non-surgical: hair loss treatment.** Assessments, scalp treatments and medical management of hair loss. Lower value per visit, but patients often start here and some move on to surgery later.

Separate campaigns let each have its own budget, ads and landing pages.

## Competing with clinics abroad

Many patients consider travelling for a cheaper procedure. Competing on price alone rarely works. What gives patients a reason to stay local:

- **Who performs the procedure,** and their qualifications and experience
- **Consultation and aftercare in person,** before and after surgery
- **Clear, complete pricing:** what is included, follow-up, and what happens if more work is needed
- **Accessible reviews and results,** where your rules allow them

Decide whether you want people searching for surgery abroad at all. Many clinics add those terms as negatives; others target them with ads that explain the case for local treatment.

## Rules that apply

- **Medicine names.** Some hair loss medicines are prescription-only, and prescription drug terms are restricted in ads, keywords and landing pages in most countries. Advertise the consultation or treatment, not the medicine
- **Claims.** Avoid promising a full head of hair, permanent results for everyone, or guaranteed graft survival
- **Before-and-after images.** Only your own patients, with consent, and only where your regulator and Google's policies allow them
- **Age rules.** In some countries, such as the UK, cosmetic intervention ads must not be directed at under-18s
- **Personalised advertising.** Google restricts targeting based on health, which can limit remarketing

[What clinics can and cannot advertise on Google Ads](/learn/healthcare-google-ads-policy-for-clinics/) explains the rules in more detail.

## Landing pages for a big decision

- The procedure and who performs it in the headline
- How the consultation works, including online assessments if you offer them
- What the procedure involves, recovery time and when results usually appear
- Price range, finance options and what is included
- Real results and reviews where allowed
- Consultation booking with a phone number and a short form

## Measure consultations and procedures

Track:

1. **Consultation requests,** including photo or online assessments
2. **Consultations attended**
3. **Procedures booked,** with their value

Patients often take months to decide, so judge campaigns over a long window. Record the source of every patient in your system and send booked procedures back to Google Ads where you can. [Enhanced conversions for leads](/learn/google-ads-enhanced-conversions-for-leads-guide/) explains how.

## Where hair clinic budgets leak

- Shampoo, supplements and hair product searches
- Natural and home remedy searches
- Medicine names and online pharmacy searches
- Hair restoration training, jobs and courses
- Surgery abroad, if you choose not to compete for it

## Frequently asked questions

### Do Google Ads work for hair transplant clinics?

Yes, when surgical and non-surgical treatment are separate campaigns, ads lead with a consultation, and the clinic tracks procedures booked. Patients research for months, so results should be judged over a long period.

### How can a local hair clinic compete with clinics abroad?

By showing what local treatment offers: a named surgeon, in-person consultation and aftercare, complete pricing and accessible reviews. Competing on price alone rarely works.

### Can hair loss clinics advertise medicine names?

Usually not, if the medicine is prescription-only. Prescription drug terms are restricted in most countries, so advertise the consultation or treatment instead.

### Are before-and-after photos allowed in hair transplant ads?

Only where your regulator and Google's policies allow them, using your own patients with consent. Check the rules before using them in ads or on landing pages.

### What should a hair transplant clinic track in Google Ads?

Consultation requests, consultations attended and procedures booked with their value. Cost per procedure booked shows profitability better than cost per enquiry.
