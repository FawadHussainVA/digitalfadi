Local customer optimization is a new Google Ads setting inside Performance Max campaigns for store goals. When it is switched on, the campaign prioritises people who are near your business, or interested in your area, and ready to act now, such as visiting a location, calling or asking for directions.

It does this by directing more budget to local formats on Google Maps, Waze and local results on Google Search. Local customer optimization only works with store goals: it cannot run in a campaign with online conversion goals or a Merchant Center product feed.

## Why Google added local customer optimization

Many businesses with physical locations want people to visit, call or get directions, not buy online. Performance Max for store goals already handles this, and local customer optimization pushes it further towards the moment someone is nearby and ready. Google announced it as one of a pair of tools for local businesses in September 2026, alongside a simpler way to bring store sales data into Google Ads through Data Manager.

## How local customer optimization works

- It sits inside **Performance Max campaigns for store goals**, which optimise for offline actions such as store visits, store sales, calls and directions
- It uses **local intent and real-time navigation signals** to find people close to or interested in your business area
- It shifts budget towards **locally tailored ads on Google Maps, Waze and local formats on Google Search**
- It works with the **locations linked to your account,** usually from your Google Business Profile

Because it relies on your location data, accuracy matters. Closed, duplicate or wrongly placed locations can send budget to the wrong places.

## What local customer optimization cannot do

- **No online goals.** It is not compatible with campaigns that optimise for online conversions
- **No Merchant Center products.** Performance Max campaigns that include a product feed cannot use local customer optimization
- **It does not replace tracking.** Store visit and store sales measurement still need to be set up and eligible in your account

If you run Performance Max with a product feed, you need a separate store goals campaign to use local customer optimization.

## How to turn on local customer optimization

**For a new campaign:**

1. Create a Performance Max campaign
2. Choose offline or store goals, such as store visits, store sales, calls or directions
3. Turn on the local customer optimization toggle when it appears in setup

**For an existing store goals campaign:** open campaign settings and switch it on under budget and bidding optimisation.

The feature has been rolling out gradually, so it may not appear in every account yet.

## Who should use local customer optimization

**Good fit:**

- Multi-location retailers, restaurants and service businesses that want visits and calls
- Single-location businesses with an accurate Business Profile and steady local demand
- Businesses where a nearby, ready-to-act customer is worth more than general awareness

**Less useful:**

- Online-only businesses
- Accounts whose main Performance Max campaign uses a product feed and online sales goals
- Businesses whose location data is out of date. Fix that first

## Before you switch it on

1. **Clean up your locations:** remove closed or duplicate listings and check addresses, pins and hours
2. **Check store goals measurement,** so you can see whether the change helps
3. **Change one thing at a time,** and compare results over several weeks rather than days
4. **Watch the reports** for which locations and formats receive spend

## Frequently asked questions

### What is local customer optimization in Google Ads?

Local customer optimization is a setting in Performance Max campaigns for store goals. It prioritises people near or interested in your business area who are ready to visit or contact you, by focusing budget on Google Maps, Waze and local Search formats.

### Which campaigns can use local customer optimization?

Only Performance Max campaigns with store goals, such as store visits, store sales, calls or directions. It cannot be used with online conversion goals or with Merchant Center products.

### How do I turn on local customer optimization?

Choose store goals when creating a Performance Max campaign and switch on the toggle in setup, or turn it on in an existing store goals campaign under budget and bidding optimisation settings.

### Does local customer optimization cost more?

It does not add a separate charge. It changes where your existing budget is focused, towards nearby, ready-to-act customers. Judge it on visits, calls and store sales over several weeks.

### Why does local customer optimization need accurate location data?

Because it decides which locations to show nearby customers based on the locations linked to your account. Closed, duplicate or misplaced listings can send budget to the wrong places.
