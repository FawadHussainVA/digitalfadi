Run Search when you need control over which searches you appear for and what your ads say, which suits most lead generation and service businesses. Run Performance Max when you sell products from a feed, have reliable conversion values and enough conversions for automation to learn from, which suits most online shops. Many accounts run both: Search for brand and core high-intent keywords, Performance Max for the product catalogue and the reach Search cannot provide.

The wrong question is which one is better. The right question is which one fits your data, your business model and how much control you need.

## The difference in one paragraph

A Search campaign shows text ads on Google search results for the keywords you choose, with ads you write and bids you control. A Performance Max campaign is a single campaign that can show ads across Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display, Discover, Gmail and Maps. You give it a goal, a budget, creative assets, a product feed if you have one, and signals about your audience. Google decides where, when and to whom the ads show.

Search gives you control and visibility. Performance Max gives you reach and automation.

## Side by side

- **Who chooses the searches:** Search, you do, through keywords. Performance Max, Google does, guided by your assets, feed and optional search themes
- **Where ads appear:** Search, on search results. Performance Max, across all of Google's channels
- **Ad copy:** Search, written by you for each ad group. Performance Max, assembled by Google from the assets you supply
- **Reporting:** Search, detailed by keyword and search term. Performance Max, improved over time but still less detailed by channel and search
- **Data it needs:** Search can work with modest conversion volume. Performance Max relies on a steady flow of accurate conversions
- **Best fit:** Search suits services, leads, local businesses and tight budgets. Performance Max suits online shops with a product feed

## When Search is the better choice

1. **You sell a service, not a catalogue.** A plumber, a law firm or a consultancy needs to appear for specific searches with specific ads. Search does exactly that
2. **Your budget is small.** Performance Max spreads spend across many channels. On a small budget, Search concentrates every click where intent is highest
3. **Conversion data is thin or unreliable.** Performance Max learns from conversions. With few, or wrong ones, it optimises towards noise
4. **You need to control the wording.** Regulated industries and businesses with strict claims rules need to know exactly what every ad says
5. **You want to see what you pay for.** Search shows every keyword, search term and bid, so problems are visible and fixable

## When Performance Max is the better choice

1. **You run an online shop with a product feed.** Performance Max is now the main way to run Shopping ads alongside other placements, and it uses your Merchant Center feed directly
2. **Conversions are accurate and frequent.** Purchases with real order values give the system something trustworthy to learn from
3. **You have creative assets.** Good images, a logo, headlines, descriptions and ideally video let it serve well across YouTube, Display and Discover rather than falling back on weak automatic assets
4. **You want reach beyond search.** Performance Max can find buyers on channels a Search campaign never touches

## Running both without them competing

Most e-commerce accounts end up with both, and that works well when each has a clear job.

- **Keep brand searches in Search.** Add your brand name as a brand exclusion in Performance Max, so cheap brand clicks do not flatter its numbers. [Should you bid on your own brand name?](/learn/should-you-bid-on-your-own-brand-name/) covers why brand traffic deserves its own campaign
- **Protect your core keywords.** When a search exactly matches a keyword in a Search campaign, the Search campaign normally takes priority over Performance Max. Keep your most important, highest-intent keywords in Search so you control their ads and bids
- **Use negatives on both sides.** Performance Max now accepts negative keywords, so the searches you have already ruled out in Search can be ruled out there too. [How to find negative keywords](/learn/how-to-find-negative-keywords/) explains where they come from
- **Judge them separately.** Compare Performance Max on non-brand results only. Its totals often include brand traffic and returning customers who would have bought anyway

## The common mistake when comparing them

The usual comparison goes like this: Performance Max shows a far better return on ad spend than Search, so the budget moves to Performance Max. Months later, total sales have not grown.

What often happened is that Performance Max took the easiest traffic, including people already searching for the brand, while Search was left with the hard, expensive auctions. Its numbers looked better because its traffic was easier, not because it was better. [Performance Max: what it hides and how to see it](/learn/performance-max-what-it-hides/) shows how to separate the two in your own account.

## How to test it fairly

If you are unsure which suits your account, test rather than assume:

1. Make sure conversion tracking counts only real sales or enquiries, with accurate values
2. Exclude your brand from Performance Max before comparing anything
3. Use a campaign experiment, or a clean before and after period of several weeks, rather than judging in the first days of learning
4. Judge on total business results, such as sales, profit or booked jobs, not on the campaign's own return on ad spend
5. Check that profit, not just revenue, improved. The [break-even ROAS calculator](/tools/break-even-roas-calculator/) shows the return you actually need

## The short answer, by business type

- **Local services and lead generation:** start with Search
- **Online shop with a good feed and accurate tracking:** Performance Max for products, Search for brand and core keywords
- **New account with little conversion history:** Search first, then add Performance Max once conversions are flowing
- **Small budget:** Search, focused on the highest-intent keywords
